# SariwaBa Development Methodology

SariwaBa uses Scrum as its primary software development methodology. Scrum is adaptive or Agile, iterative, and incremental. The team organizes project work through a prioritized product backlog and a sequence of two-week sprints.

## Development Workflow

1. Record features, technical tasks, identified bugs, and documentation requirements in the product backlog.
2. Select priority backlog items and define a sprint goal during sprint planning.
3. Confirm requirements and acceptance criteria before implementation.
4. Design, develop, test, review, and integrate the selected work during the sprint.
5. Demonstrate the working increment during the sprint review.
6. Record feedback from the instructor, adviser, and intended users.
7. Conduct a retrospective and prioritize approved changes for the next sprint.
8. Deploy accepted milestones using Vercel, Render, and the Android APK distribution process.

Noncritical changes will not interrupt the active sprint. They will be recorded in the product backlog and considered during the next sprint-planning session.

## Methodology Records

- `development-methodology-plan.pdf` – completed Activity 4 plan and worksheets
- `methodology-selection-justification.md` – explanation of the Scrum selection
- `sprint-or-iteration-notes/` – sprint goals, completed work, and retrospective notes
- `review-and-feedback-records/` – stakeholder feedback, decisions, and approvals
- `test-evidence/` – test results, screenshots, and reports
- `release-notes/` – milestone and final-release summaries
